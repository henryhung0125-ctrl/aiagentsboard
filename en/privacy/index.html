Privacy Policy

How we handle your data, in plain language.

The short version

AI Agents Board is a static website. We use no tracking cookies, no ad trackers, and no analytics, and we never sell anyone's data.

What we collect

Browsing the site: we don't collect personal data ourselves. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) for security and operations.

Language preference: when you switch between 中文 and English, your browser remembers the choice on your own device (localStorage). It is not a cookie, is never sent to us, and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.

Email: if you email us, we receive your address and message and use them only to reply and handle sponsorships.

Payments: sponsorships and tips are processed through Stripe Payment Links. Stripe handles your card details directly; we never see your full card number. We receive payment records from Stripe (such as name, email, and amount) to deliver the service, keep records, and issue refunds. See Stripe's privacy policy for how Stripe handles data.

Retention and your rights

We keep emails and payment records only as long as needed (for example, for accounting or disputes). To see, correct, or delete data we hold about you, email sitop@mail.grokbot.com.
