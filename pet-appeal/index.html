Pet insurance claim denied? We write your appeal letter.

Your dog or cat had treatment, the bill is large, and the insurer said no. Send us the denial letter, the policy if you have it, and the vet records. We'll read them, look for where the denial is weak, and write a ready-to-send appeal letter in your name, plus a short checklist of what to attach and where to send it.

US$79

Per claim, after a free first review. If we see no reasonable appeal ground, you pay nothing.

Send a free first review

How it works

  1. Send a free first review: the denial letter, the policy if you have it, vet records or invoices, and a short account of what happened.
  2. We read the policy and the denial reason, and look for weak points — for example pre-existing condition timing, waiting periods, exclusion wording, missing records, or claim coding.
  3. If we see a reasonable ground for an appeal, we quote you. The price is US$79 per claim. If we don't, we tell you and you pay nothing.
  4. After you pay, we deliver a ready-to-send letter in your name and a short checklist. We aim to do this in about 2 business days. That's a target, not a guarantee. You send the letter yourself.

What you get

  • A ready-to-send appeal letter in your name
  • A short checklist of what to attach and where and how to send it

What this is not

We are not a law firm, not veterinarians, and not affiliated with any insurer. This is not legal or veterinary advice. We can't guarantee the insurer will reverse the decision. You send the letter yourself.

FAQ

Which insurers?

Any US pet insurer, for example Trupanion, Nationwide, Healthy Paws, Lemonade, Embrace, Pets Best, or ASPCA.

What if I already appealed once?

You can still send us the case. We'll say in the free review whether another letter is likely to help. If we don't see a reasonable ground, you pay nothing.

What do I need?

The denial letter, the policy if you have it, vet records or invoices, and a short account of what happened. If a file is missing, say so in the form and send what you have.

How do I pay?

After the free review, if we can write an appeal, we send a payment link. There's nothing to pay on this page.

Is my information safe?

We only use your documents to review the denial and write your appeal. We don't use them for anything else. If you ask us to delete them, we will.
